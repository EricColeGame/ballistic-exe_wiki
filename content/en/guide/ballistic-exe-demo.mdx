### Advanced Movement Techniques

1. **Slide-Jumping**: Trigger a slide while at top sprint velocity, then immediately jump within the first half-second of the animation to carry forward momentum across gaps.
2. **Wall-Rebound**: Colliding with vertical geometry while airborne lets you initiate a lateral leap, throwing off enemy tracking and granting height advantages.
3. **Corner Peeking with Crouch Cancels**: Tap crouch while rounding corners to drop your hitbox below headshot level while maintaining forward trajectory.

Community reports emphasize that lowering mouse smoothing and keeping raw input enabled in your configuration files makes twitch-aiming drastically more consistent.

## Tactical Combat Tips for Completing the Demo Trial

Surviving elite encounters demands thoughtful resource management rather than reckless rushing. Keep the following battlefield rules in mind during your playthrough:

- **Never Stop Moving**: Static players are easy targets. AI routines are built to suppress stationary positions with tracking projectiles and flanking paths.
- **Reload During Traversal**: Perform reloads while sliding behind cover or mid-air between platforms so your offensive output resumes the instant you land.
- **Monitor Audio Cues**: Distinct audio indicators telegraph heavy enemy attacks, allowing you to dash sideways before lethal strikes connect.
- **Control the Arena Perimeter**: Hug the outer edges of combat arenas to keep threats in front of you, reducing the chance of being blindsided from behind.
