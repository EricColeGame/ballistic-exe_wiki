## Preparing for Launch: Tips for Prospective Players

Entering a high-speed horror shooter without preparation can lead to quick frustration. Drawing from community reports and combat patterns showcased so far, adopting the right mindset early will drastically smooth your initial learning curve:

- **Configure FOV Early**: High-speed slide mechanics require a wide field of view (typically 100° to 110°) to keep peripheral enemies in sight during circular strafes.
- **Master Audio Spatialization**: Audio cues signal off-screen projectile launches and glitch entity spawns long before visual telegraphs appear on screen. Use quality stereo headphones.
- **Resist the Urge to Backpedal**: Combat arenas penalize players who retreat into corners. Move laterally around threats while keeping your sights locked on critical weak points.
- **Study Terminal Sequences**: Interacting with stage computers while dodging incoming fire is key to surviving boss battle phases. Practice executing interface commands under pressure.
