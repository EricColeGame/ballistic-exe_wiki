2. **Run the Built-in Offset Wizard:**  
   Launch the sound menu and execute at least 20 test taps to establish your base hardware latency. Wireless headphones often introduce 40–80 ms of latency, so wired audio gear is strongly advised.

   Faster scroll speeds actually increase note spacing on screen, making dense clusters significantly easier to read. Most skilled players set their scroll rate between `2.6` and `3.2`.
